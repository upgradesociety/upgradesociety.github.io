Upgrade Society is an independent project exploring how society could become more cooperative, regenerative, intelligent and free.

Rather than focusing only on criticism, Upgrade Society is primarily interested in **construction**:

* new forms of community and cooperation;
* more resilient local economies;
* regenerative food, land and energy systems;
* new models of housing and coliving;
* tools that give individuals and communities greater autonomy;
* better ways to coordinate people, knowledge and resources;
* economic systems better suited to automation and artificial intelligence;
* institutions designed around human flourishing rather than permanent competition.

Ideas can begin as research, writing or scenarios and later become real experiments, software, events, communities or physical spaces.

The approach is simple:

**understand → imagine → experiment → improve**

## About CitizenUpgrade

CitizenUpgrade is the public identity of [CitizenPedro](https://primal.net/p/nprofile1qqsx9f4yrchslq3ytp3pt08zrwx5k8rlgxrz9ck5wm3e0rw6ky4vkscedtmk0), a person from Portugal exploring how individuals, technology and society can evolve together.

Pedro's interests cross philosophy, engineering, artificial intelligence, community-building, regeneration and the design of better social systems. A recurring theme is the connection between fields that are usually treated separately: inner development and social change, technology and nature, individual freedom and cooperation, ideas and practical experimentation.

CitizenPedro is interested in **integrative and non-dualistic philosophy**: moving beyond rigid ideological camps and trying to understand apparently opposing perspectives as parts of a larger system.

The goal is not to remove differences or avoid disagreement, but to understand what is valuable in different perspectives and combine them into something more complete.

CitizenUpgrade is also intentionally unfinished. It reflects a person, a philosophy and a body of projects that are still evolving.

## Direction

The long-term goal is to connect **people, ideas, technology and places** that can experiment with better ways of living.

Not one ideology. Not one blueprint.

**A society capable of improving itself.**
