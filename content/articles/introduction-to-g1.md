# Ğ1 (June) — Introduction to Ğ1

---

## Part I — Foundations of the Libre Currency

### 1. Concept and Origins

- Ğ1 (“June”) is the first real-world currency based on the *Théorie Relative de la Monnaie* (TRM), created by Stéphane Laborde.  
- Its core idea is **freedom in money creation**: every person creates money equally, over time.

**Simple idea:**  
> **“Ğ1 is a money where everyone receives new money regularly, just for being alive.”**

In Ğ1, money creation does not depend on:
- having a job  
- owning capital  
- getting a loan  
- approval from banks or the state  

It depends only on **being a human participant in time**.

- Comparison with other systems:
  - **Bitcoin** → open software, but money creation favors machines and early users  
  - **Fiat money** → created by banks and states, through debt  
  - **Ğ1** → created equally by humans  

**Important clarification:**  
Ğ1 does not try to control prices or make things scarce.  
Its goal is simpler: **fair money creation between people and generations**.

**Learn more (overview & portals):**
- https://www.infojune.fr/  
- https://g1currency.org/

### 2. The Universal Dividend (UD)

- The basic unit of money creation in Ğ1 is the **Universal Dividend (UD)**.  
- The UD is calculated from the **average money per person (M/N)**.  
- Every certified member receives it regularly.  
- This creates fairness:
  - between people alive today  
  - between people born earlier or later  
- The UD is mainly a **way to measure value**, not a way to store wealth.

**Concrete example:**  
At current levels, each certified member receives about:
- ~10 Ğ1 per day  
- ~300 Ğ1 per month  
- ~3,650 Ğ1 per year  

This money:
- appears automatically  
- is the same for everyone  
- does not depend on work, income, or status  

**What it feels like in practice:**
- New money appears in your wallet every day  
- You don’t apply  
- You don’t owe anything  
- You don’t need permission  

The UD is **not a salary**, **not welfare**, and **not charity**.  
It simply means: *you are part of the economy*.

**Life example:**

**Alice joins at 20**
- Uses Ğ1 for 60 years  
- Creates about **219,000 Ğ1** total  

**Bob joins at 60**
- Uses Ğ1 for 20 years  
- Creates about **73,000 Ğ1** total  

➡ Bob creates less money overall  
➡ But **while alive**, Bob receives the **same share as everyone else**

The UD makes people **equal at the same time**, not forever.

---

### 3. Relative Money Theory (TRM)

- Money is a **relative measuring tool**, like time or distance  
- Prices change because the reference point changes  
- Human life has limits, so money must scale with time  
- TRM is both:
  - a mathematical idea  
  - an ethical idea about fairness  

**Simple way to think about it:**
- 3,000 Ğ1 ≈ about 10 months of UD  
- 30,000 Ğ1 ≈ about 100 months of UD  

➡ What matters is **economic time**, not raw numbers  
➡ Old money does not give endless power  

**Learn TRM and simulations:**
- https://trm.creationmonetaire.info/  
- https://www.creationmonetaire.info/

## Part II — Technical Architecture

### 4. Duniter Blockchain

Ğ1 runs on **Duniter**, an open-source peer-to-peer blockchain  
- It manages:
  - block creation  
  - member verification  
  - UD generation  

- Security uses a **Web of Trust (WoT)** to prevent fake identities  

**Key point:**  
The fairness rules are enforced **by software**, not by politicians.

- Duniter is evolving toward **Duniter V2**, with:
  - better scalability  
  - a new forger system  
  - modular governance  

**Technical resources:**
- https://duniter.org/  
- https://github.com/duniter  

---

### 5. Wallets and Interfaces

- **Cesium** is the main wallet and identity app  
- Works on:
  - mobile  
  - web  
  - desktop  

Two main user types:
- **Co-creator wallets** → certified members creating the UD  
- **Simple wallets** → users who just send and receive Ğ1  

- Cesium+ adds profiles, ads, and marketplaces

### 6. Web of Trust and Identity Verification

- Each person must be certified by **at least 5 people**  
- Certifications expire to keep the network healthy  
- Prevents one person from creating many fake accounts  

- The network can be copied (forked):
  - Ğ2, Ğ3, etc.

## Part III — Economic Logic and Comparison

### 7. Inflation and Monetary Fairness

- Ğ1 does **not create inflation** like fiat money  
- Everyone’s balance grows at the same rate  
- Prices move, but fairness remains  

Inflation in fiat systems comes from **unequal money creation**.

---

### 8. Valuation and Mixed Pricing

- Prices often combine Ğ1 and euros  
- In theory, currencies are not convertible by formula  
- In practice, people still find working habits  

**Observed reality:**
- Around **1 € ≈ 3–5 UD**

**Example:**
- Rent: 500 €  
- Food & services: 200 € + 300 Ğ1  

This is a **transition phase**, not a contradiction.

---

### 9. Comparison with Other Systems

- **Bitcoin:** limited supply, favors early users  
- **LETS:** local exchange, no universal creation  
- **Fiat money:** debt-based, centralized  
- **Ğ1:** decentralized, human-created, fair over time
