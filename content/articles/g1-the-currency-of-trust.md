## 2. The G1 Revolution — Money Without Masters

**G1** (pronounced “Gee-One”) is a *mutual credit currency*:  
a system where money is not issued by a bank, but by the **people themselves**, in direct proportion to their cooperation.  

When two members trade, **new credit is created symmetrically**:  
- One account goes +10 G1.  
- The other goes −10 G1.  

No one “prints” money.  
No one pays interest.  
No one is enslaved.  

The total sum of the system is always zero — pure reciprocity.  
This means the **currency grows with trust, not debt.**  

It’s money that mirrors moral relationships.  
Each trade is both accounting and acknowledgment:  
> “I trust your competence enough to owe you.”  

In a world poisoned by abstract finance, G1 restores **human-scale credit** — transparent, voluntary, and regenerative.
