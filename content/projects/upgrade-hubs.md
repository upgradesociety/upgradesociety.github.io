# Upgrade Hubs  
## A Network for New Technologies, Economies, Communities, and Regenerative Living

Upgrade Hubs are **temporary pop-up co-living gatherings** in depopulating areas where people come together to experiment with new ways of living, working, and exchanging value.

Participants include:

- digital nomads  
- builders and entrepreneurs  
- permaculture practitioners  
- artists and musicians  
- technologists  
- people interested in alternative & regenerative economies  

These gatherings combine **co-living, learning, culture, and economic experimentation**.

The long-term goal is to create a **network of communities and economies that increase freedom, cooperation, and sustainability.**

---

# 1. The Core Idea

Upgrade Hubs are **temporary network nodes** that bring people together to:

- build relationships  
- learn new systems  
- experiment with co-created economies  
- explore regenerative living  

They function as a mix of:

- co-living
- hackathon
- cultural gathering
- village laboratory

The hub acts as a **gateway into a wider network**.

---

# 2. What Happens at an Upgrade Hub

A hub typically lasts **1–4 weeks** and includes:

## Workshops and Talks

Topics may include:

- Bitcoin and decentralized finance  
- co-created money systems  
- startups and entrepreneurship  
- AI and technology tools  
- cooperative economics  

## Collaborative Work

Participants may:

- work remotely
- build startup ideas
- code together
- collaborate on projects

## Regenerative Living

Activities may include:

- permaculture workshops  
- food production  
- farm visits  
- ecological design  

## Arts and Culture

Evenings often include:

- music jams  
- film nights  
- philosophy discussions  
- shared meals  

The hub creates **community and culture**, not just education.

---

# 3. The Three-Layer Ecosystem

The Upgrade Hub model creates a **three-layer system**.

## Layer 1 — Social Layer

The **human network**.

Created through:

- gatherings  
- co-living experiences  
- friendships  
- shared projects  

This builds **trust and identity**.

---

## Layer 2 — Economic Layer

The **co-created money network**.

Participants experiment with:

- mutual credit systems  
- universal dividend currencies  
- decentralized payment networks  

This enables exchange without relying only on traditional money.

---

## Layer 3 — Infrastructure Layer

The **physical nodes**.

Over time the network grows into:

- co-living houses  
- farms  
- maker spaces  
- studios  
- rural villages  

These become **permanent nodes in the network**.

---

# 4. How the System Grows

The progression looks like this:

Upgrade Hub Event  
↓  
Community Formation  
↓  
Co-Created Currency Use  
↓  
Local Economic Activity  
↓  
Permanent Nodes and Villages


Hubs act as **seeds for long-term communities**.

---

# 5. Why Depopulating Areas

Depopulating rural areas are ideal for Upgrade Hubs.

They typically offer:

- affordable housing  
- unused buildings  
- welcoming municipalities  
- natural environments  
- low living costs  

These conditions make it possible to experiment with new communities.

---

# 6. Duration of Hubs

Different formats are possible.

## Short Hub

**7 days**

A festival-like gathering.

---

## Medium Hub

**2–3 weeks**

The most effective format.

Participants build relationships and collaborate.

---

## Long Hub

**1–3 months**

Creates a temporary village.

Often leads to long-term projects.

---

# 7. Economic Vision

The hubs introduce participants to **co-created money systems**.

Examples include:

- mutual credit networks  
- universal dividend currencies  
- hybrid economic models  

The goal is to create **parallel local economies** that support cooperation and accessibility.

---

# 8. A Hybrid Economic Model

A powerful structure combines multiple monetary layers.

Bitcoin → long-term savings  
Network Currency → daily economy  
Euro → bridge to traditional markets


This structure allows:

- stability
- accessibility
- experimentation

---

# 9. Network Identity and Trust

Trust is essential for decentralized systems.

Possible mechanisms include:

- web-of-trust identity verification  
- participation in hubs  
- reputation systems  
- community attestations  

Participation in hubs strengthens **social trust within the network**.

---

# 10. Growth Strategy (10-Year Vision)

## Phase 1 — Seeding (Years 1–2)

Goal:

100–300 participants.

Actions:

- organize initial hubs  
- onboard people to the network  
- experiment with small markets  

---

## Phase 2 — Network Formation (Years 3–5)

Goal:

1,000–5,000 participants.

New developments:

- services exchanged within the network  
- small businesses emerging  
- shared infrastructure  

The currency becomes **practically useful**.

---

## Phase 3 — Ecosystem (Years 5–10)

Goal:

10,000–50,000 participants.

At this stage the network supports:

- housing projects  
- food systems  
- remote work networks  
- cultural communities  

The system becomes a **parallel cooperative economy**.

---

# 11. The Role of Upgrade Hubs

Upgrade Hubs solve three major problems that alternative economic systems often face.

## Adoption

People join the network through **events and community experiences**.

---

## Liquidity

Local markets and exchanges create **real economic activity**.

---

## Culture

Music, food, collaboration, and friendship create **shared identity**.

---

# 12. Long-Term Vision

The long-term goal is a **global network of hubs and communities**.

Example network:

Portugal Hub  
Spain Hub  
Italy Hub  
Romania Hub  
Argentina Hub


Each hub connects people into the same ecosystem.

---

# 13. Core Principles

The Upgrade Hub network is based on:

- voluntary cooperation  
- open participation  
- decentralized organization  
- regenerative living  
- technological innovation  

The goal is to build **communities that increase freedom, resilience, and well-being**.

---

# 14. A Protocol, Not a Company

The network should function like a **protocol**.

Meaning:

- anyone can host a hub  
- anyone can start a node  
- anyone can join the network  

The system grows through **distributed initiative**, not central control.

---

# 15. The Ultimate Purpose

Upgrade Hubs aim to create systems that:

- reduce suffering  
- increase cooperation  
- empower individuals and communities  
- regenerate local economies  
- foster meaningful human connection  

Through small gatherings and networks of trust, it becomes possible to **experiment with better ways of living together**.
