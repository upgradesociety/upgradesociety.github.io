## Co-Created Money

Money creation, universal dividends, Ğ1 and experiments in human-centered monetary systems.

- **[History of Money](/articles/the-core-idea-co-created-money/)** — A historical introduction to the recurring idea that communities can create exchange media themselves through mutual credit and related systems.
- **[What is Co-Created Money](/articles/what-is-co-created-money/)** — The clearest conceptual introduction: instead of redistributing money after creation, participants share equally in creating it from the beginning.
- **[The Universal Dividend — How Money Is Created](/ideas/the-universal-dividend/)** — A focused explanation of Ğ1’s Universal Dividend: every human participant creates money equally through time.
- **[Ğ1 (June) — Introduction to Ğ1](/articles/introduction-to-g1/)** — A practical introduction to Ğ1, the Relative Theory of Money and human-based issuance compared with fiat and Bitcoin.
- **[The Libre Money Experiment, Ğ1 and the Economics of Universal Dividend](/articles/the-libre-money-experiment-g1-and-the-economics-of-universal-dividend/)** — A compact technical outline of Ğ1, the Universal Dividend, monetary symmetry and the Relative Theory of Money.
- **[The Libre Currency: Ğ1 and the Future of Human-Centered Money](/guides/the-libre-currency-g1/)** — The deepest existing guide to Ğ1, covering its monetary theory, implementation, Web of Trust and broader philosophy.
- **[G1 — The Currency of Trust](/articles/g1-the-currency-of-trust/)** — A moral and institutional argument for Ğ1 as money built around trust rather than debt-based dependence.

## The World Now

Diagnosis of the pressures shaping everyday life: money, work, generational change, conflict and institutional strain.

- **[Why the Fiat System Is Useless for the Working Class](/articles/why-the-fiat-system-is-useless-for-the-working-class/)** — An argument that financialized fiat money has broken the connection between work, saving and long-term security for ordinary earners.
- **[The Problem Is Simpler Than We Think](/articles/the-problem-is-simpler-than-we-think/)** — A structural diagnosis that traces housing, wages and credit problems back to how new money is created and who gets first access to it.
- **[Why the Millennials Will Lead the New Change](/articles/why-the-millennials-will-lead-the-new-change/)** — A generational argument that people promised stability but given debt, inflation and insecurity may become the builders of new institutions.
- **[Why Is There So Much Conflict and Trauma in the World?](/articles/why-is-there-so-much-conflict-and-trauma-in-the-world/)** — A structural exploration of conflict, resentment and burnout through powerlessness, monetary systems and institutional design.

## Nation States

How nation-states, public finance and large-scale political order may change through the coming transition.

- **[From 2025 to 2050 — The World in Transition](/articles/from-2025-to-2050-the-world-in-transition/)** — A future scenario in which nation-states remain but everyday life becomes more local, regional and networked as centralized legitimacy weakens.
- **[BitBonds](/articles/bitbonds/)** — A speculative proposal for euro-denominated bonds collateralized by Bitcoin as a bridge between hard digital collateral and public finance.

## Cooperatives

Cooperatives, upgraded communities, hubs and local economic institutions that can be built in practice.

- **[The Cooperative Century](/guides/the-cooperative-century/)** — A multi-decade roadmap from small cooperatives and experimental currencies toward a mature civic and cooperative economy.
